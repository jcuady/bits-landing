## Overview

BITScrm is the mission-critical debt recovery platform purpose-built exclusively for collection agencies, debt recovery law firms, consumer lending desks, and recovery BPOs. It is **not** a general sales CRM.

BITScrm unifies delinquent account staging, aging bucket distribution, browser WebRTC softphones, automated Promise-to-Pay (PTP) scheduling, supervisor QA HUD, and BSP Circulars 454/857 compliance.

---

## Core Operational Pillars

### 1. Portfolio & Queue Intelligence
- Dynamic debtor segmentation by delinquency bucket (1-30, 31-60, 60+ DPD).
- Automated queue assignment based on agent skill and historical recovery success rate.
- Controlled bulk imports with CSV validation and duplicate record reconciliation.

### 2. Embedded WebRTC Softphone & Auto-Dialer
- In-browser SIP softphone with zero desktop installations required.
- Power, preview, and predictive dialing modes with disposition logging.
- Call recording with automatic encryption and configurable compliance retention.

### 3. Automated Promise-to-Pay (PTP) Engine
- PTP scheduling with installment calculations, payment method capture, and automated debtor reminders.
- Broken PTP detection: automatically re-queues defaulting accounts to priority queues.
- Real-time payment reconciliation against bank and payment gateway reports.

### 4. Supervisor Quality Assurance & Live HUD
- Real-time call monitoring HUD: live listen, whisper coaching, and barge-in functionality.
- Agent performance metrics: right-party connect rate, call duration, PTP conversion rate.
- Standardized QA scorecards with call recording playback for supervisory evaluation.

## Regulatory Compliance & Security

- **BSP Circulars 454 & 857**: Fair debt collection practices, strict contact hour limits (6:00 AM to 10:00 PM), and anti-harassment safeguards.
- **NPC RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)**: Encrypted debtor storage, explicit consent tracking, DSAR compliance.
- **Enterprise RBAC**: Role-based access control isolating debtor PII, financial amounts, and supervisor overrides.
- **WORM Audit Trails**: Write-Once-Read-Many audit logs recording every disposition, view, and payment attempt.
