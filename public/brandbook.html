Social Media Copywriting Rules

✓ Approved Copy Formula

  • 1. Lead with the Floor Problem: "Your agents are spending 45% of shift time dialing dead numbers."
  • 2. Present the Engineering Fix: "BITScrm automates queue routing and filters answering machines in under 300ms."
  • 3. Show Concrete Evidence: "Result: 3.2x higher Right-Party Connect rates without adding seats."
  • 4. Clean Direct CTA: "Request architecture consultation at boundlessits.com"

✗ Banned Copy Clichés

  • No Vague Hype: Never say "revolutionize your business", "supercharge your synergy", or "unleash AI magic".
  • No Emoji Spam: Ban emoji overload (🚀🔥💥🤖). Keep posts clean and corporate.
  • No Fake Guarantees: Never promise "100% debt recovery guaranteed" or legal advice.
  • No Dark Gimmicks: No generic purple AI neon glows or misleading screenshots.
07 · Digital Standards

Website & UI Component Rules

Strict front-end standards ensuring tactile micro-interactions, responsive mobile stability, and WCAG accessibility across all web touchpoints.

Buttons & Targets

Touch targets must always be at least 44 × 44 px. Primary buttons use rounded-full pill shape with electric blue fill.

Form & Inputs

Labels sit strictly above inputs. Form text inputs must be at least 16px to prevent automatic iOS Safari viewport zoom.

Cards & Materiality

Use light mode only. Standardize borders to 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.9) with 16-24px border radius.

✓ Subtle shadow-xs elevation
✓ 0% dark surface neon glows
✓ No redundant cards-in-cards
