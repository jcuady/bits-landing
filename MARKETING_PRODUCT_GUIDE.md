### FLAGSHIP 1: BITScrm Collections (`collections`)
*Primary Route: `/bitscrm` · Deep-Dive Route: `/products/collections`*

#### What It Does (In Depth)
BITScrm Collections is our crown-jewel flagship enterprise software. Engineered specifically for high-volume debt recovery agencies, BPO contact centers, consumer lenders, fintech microfinance institutions, and auto-loan recovery desks. It centralizes delinquent account management from 1-30 DPD (Days Past Due) through 180+ DPD write-offs. 

It features an integrated browser WebRTC SIP softphone with predictive and campaign dialing, eliminating manual dial errors. Supervisors have a real-time floor HUD allowing live listen, whisper coaching, and call barging. Built-in compliance locks enforce statutory calling hours (preventing illegal night-time harassment) and mandate BSP Circulars 454 and 857 compliance.

#### The Core Problem It Solves
- **Spreadsheet Paralysis**: Delinquency portfolios managed across disjointed Excel files cause broken Promise-to-Pay (PTP) commitments to be forgotten.
- **Dialing Inefficiency**: Collectors manually punching phone numbers waste up to 45 minutes of every hour on busy signals, unanswered lines, and voicemail.
- **Compliance Exposure**: Heavy regulatory penalties from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) or National Privacy Commission (NPC) due to unmonitored collector behavior or improper borrower disclosures.

#### Key Engineered Capabilities
- **Dynamic Account Staging & Aging Buckets**: Automated movement across DPD delinquency tiers (Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ DPD).
- **WebRTC Browser SIP Softphone & Dialer**: Zero hardware needed; progressive and preview auto-dialing directly inside Chrome/Edge.
- **Automated PTP Scheduling & Tracking**: Sets automated payment reminders, tracks broken arrangements, and recalculates penalty interest.
- **Supervisor Live HUD (Listen, Whisper, Barge)**: Quality assurance managers can silently monitor junior agents, whisper guidance into their headset without the debtor hearing, or barge in to close agreements.
- **WORM Audit Trail & Audio Archival**: Tamper-resistant Write-Once-Read-Many audit logs satisfying legal evidentiary standards.

#### Key Metrics & Social Proof
- **3.2x Boost** in Right-Party Connect (RPC) rates.
- **42% Reduction** in broken Promise-to-Pay default rates.
- **Saves 2.5 hours** of manual administrative logging per collector daily.
- **100% BSP & NPC RA 10173 DPA** regulatory compliance alignment.

#### Target Buyer Personas
- Collection Agency CEOs & Managing Directors
- Chief Operating Officers (COO) of BPO Contact Centers
- Heads of Credit & Collections at Consumer Banks & Fintechs
- Legal & Compliance Officers overseeing loan recovery

#### Sample Ad Angles & Hooks

### 1. BITS Pickleball & Court Operating System (`pickleball`)
*Route: `/products/pickleball`*
*(NEW FLAGSHIP PRODUCT IN THE SPORTS CATEGORY)*

#### What It Does (In Depth)
The dedicated court operating system built specifically for the booming pickleball and padel industry. It handles two simultaneous operational models:
1. **Open-Play Paddle Queuing**: Replaces messy chalkboards and physical paddle racks with an automated digital rotation queue. It supports standard rotation rules:
   - **4-On / 4-Off**: When a match concludes, all 4 players rotate off and the next 4 players from the digital paddle rack take the court.
   - **Winner Stays (Max 2)**: Winners stay on court, split partners, and play against the top 2 players waiting in the rack.
   - **DUPR / Skill-Tier Matching**: Groups players by rating (e.g., 3.0, 3.5, 4.0+) so games stay competitive.
2. **Online Court Reservations & Instant Deposit Booking**: 24/7 calendar availability allowing players to book private courts online with automated security deposits, eliminating no-shows.
3. **Overhead TV Screen HUD & Audio Chimes**: Live overhead TV monitors tell players exactly which court has opened up and sounds an automated chime (*"Next 4 players, please proceed to Court 2"*).

#### The Core Problem It Solves
- **Whiteboard Chaos & Queue Arguments**: Open-play rotations on manual whiteboards lead to angry shouting matches over who has next court or who stayed too long.
- **Empty Courts & Lost Revenue**: Venue owners suffer thousands of pesos in lost court rental fees due to manual phone booking double-bookings and no-shows.
- **Front-Desk Overhead**: Desk staff are overwhelmed answering calls, taking down reservations, collecting cash, and shouting across courts to direct players.

#### Key Engineered Capabilities
- **Digital Paddle Rack**: Scan mobile QR code to drop paddle into the queue.
- **Overhead TV Screen Wall HUD**: Real-time display of court statuses, active scores, and next-up players.
- **Audio Chime System**: Automates court announcements across facility speakers.
- **Integrated Booking & Payment Escrow**: Online checkout with Maya, GCash, or card.
- **Player Skill Balancing**: Native integration with DUPR ratings or internal club tiers.

#### Killer Metric & Benchmark
- **99.4% Peak Court Utilization** during evening and weekend open-play slots.
- **Zero Queue Arguments** recorded across partner venues.

#### Target Buyer Personas
- Pickleball & Padel Club Owners & Facility General Managers
- Country Club Athletic Directors & Sports Complex Operators
- Tournament Organizers & Racket Sport Facility Investors
